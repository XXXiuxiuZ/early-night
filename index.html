    Your steps

    These are the pieces of your circle, in clockwise order. Tap an emoji to change it. The last step decides whether the night counts as early, so new steps go just before it.

    Reminders on iPhone

    Open the app automatically with Shortcuts:

    1. Open the Shortcuts app, tap Automation, then New Automation.
    2. Choose Time of Day, pick your wind-down time, repeat daily.
    3. Select Run Immediately so it doesn’t ask each time.
    4. Add the action Open URL with this app’s address.
